About Baymont

Baymont Cape Projects is a Western Cape electrical contractor specialising in the maintenance, repair, installation and commissioning of medium-voltage (MV) and low-voltage (LV) infrastructure, including substations, transformers up to 33 kV, switchgear, protection systems and standby generators.

Established in 1997, our team of 33 staff is backed by a fully equipped workshop, substation and cable test equipment, crane trucks and our own vehicle fleet.

Our services

MV & LV Substations
  • Transformers up to 33 kV
  • MV & LV switchgear (oil, gas and vacuum)
  • Substation protection devices and relays
  • Change-over panels
  • Battery tripping units and chargers
Testing & Diagnostics
  • Protection relay testing (secondary injection)
  • Infra-red scanning with reporting
  • Drone thermal imaging on overhead lines and solar fields
  • Data logging
  • Cable fault location and repair
Generators
  • All generator makes and models
  • Controllers, changeover and sync panels
  • Generator and alternator refurbishment
  • Service, hire and diesel filling
  • Temporary LV panels
Oil & Diesel Services
  • Transformer oil and diesel sampling
  • On-site transformer oil purification and regeneration
  • Diesel purification
Power Factor & Automation
  • Power factor correction, incl. detuned capacitor banks
  • PLC and automation (Delta PLCs and HMIs)
Manufacturing, Civil & Cleaning
  • Panel manufacturing in our plate metal workshop
  • In-house casting of transformer, switchgear and minisub plinths
  • Dry ice blasting
